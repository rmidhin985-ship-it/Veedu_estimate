House-work scope checklist

Review against the drawings and agreed scope. A listed item does not mean the whole stage is included. Some works may not apply.

  • Site clearance, excavation, disposal, filling and compaction
  • Foundation masonry / concrete; damp-proofing and treatment where specified
  • RCC footings, columns, beams, slabs and stairs; steel from BBS and formwork separately
  • Wall masonry, lintels, sunshades and parapets
  • Roofing, waterproofing and rainwater drainage
  • Plaster, screed, floor/wall tiles, skirting and stone finishes
  • Frames, shutters, glazing, hardware, grills and handrails
  • Selected putty, primer, paint and wood finishing
  • Water supply, drainage, fixtures, tanks and sewage treatment
  • Electrical wiring, DB, earthing, switches and fixtures
  • External drains, paving, compound wall, gate and site-specific works

Use separate measured items or Custom Item for works without a dedicated template. Complete-supply items must not overlap their separately measured components. Direct-rate items do not supply a material breakup.

How to Use

  1. Enter project details.
  2. Select a work item, then assign its floor / location. Foundation and shared works can be kept under Foundation / Common. Existing projects without floor labels open as Unassigned.
  3. Use Specification, Measurements, Rate Analysis and Item Total shortcuts within the item editor. These keep all details on one page.
  4. Add measurement rows. Use “Deduct” for openings. A Direct Quantity option is also available.
  5. Material quantities update automatically when specification inputs change. Enter supplier rates, wastage, labour and other costs. Open Advanced to edit coefficients.
  6. Review floor subtotals, BOQ and material abstract. Choose All floors for a consolidated report, or select one floor. Print / PDF and BOQ CSV follow the selected report scope. The dashboard always shows the whole project.
  7. Download the project to continue later. Use “Open Project” to reopen it.

Sample rates are examples only and are not official schedule rates. Review quantity and rate analysis whenever the specification changes.
